**Featured projects**

**[Did Streaming Make Songs Shorter?](https://github.com/jlsalter/hit-length):** An analysis of every Billboard Hot 100 hit since 1958, built with SQL and Python. Hit songs have been getting shorter since 1992, but the decline sped up about four times after 2016, when streaming took over. It happened across genres and on album tracks, and shorter songs started staying on the chart longer. Includes an [interactive explorer](https://jlsalter.github.io/hit-length/) of all 24,000+ songs.

**[The ABS Effect](https://github.com/jlsalter/abs-challenge-analysis):** An analysis of MLB's 2026 automated ball-strike challenge system using 1.4 million Statcast pitches. It measures how umpires adapted (the called strike zone shrank by 35 square inches, 95% CI: 29–40) and models when a challenge is worth making, estimating that better decisions would add about 1.5 wins per team per season.

**[Pitch Design Lab](https://github.com/jlsalter/pitch-design-lab):** Stuff+, Location+, and Pitching+ models built with gradient boosting on 1.4 million MLB pitches, then used to recommend specific changes for individual pitchers. I tested the recommendations on 174 pitchers who changed their pitches between seasons, and their results moved the way the model predicted.
